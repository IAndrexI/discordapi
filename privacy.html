Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 • chat.protutech.vip

1. The Zero-Knowledge Principle

Protutech is designed around a fundamental core privacy premise: Your sensitive authentication tokens and session credentials must never be retained on a central server.

All Discord user tokens, Matrix session keys, and custom plugin configurations are kept exclusively inside your local device's isolated client sandbox (window.localStorage or your local operating system user profile).

2. What Information is Processed

To deliver real-time voice, video, and chat, the infrastructure processes:

  • WebRTC Voice & Video Streams: Handled in-memory by our on-premises LiveKit SFU (Selective Forwarding Unit) with end-to-end encryption capability. Voice data is never recorded, saved to disk, or analyzed.
  • Matrix Chat Messages: Encrypted chat messages synchronized across room participants according to standard Matrix protocol specifications.
  • Ephemeral Session Tokens: Time-limited JWTs signed for LiveKit SFU access to authenticate voice room participation (valid for 24 hours).

3. What Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe data minimization is the best form of privacy. The Protutech server:

  • Does NOT log or persist Discord account passwords or MFA secrets.
  • Does NOT save Discord user tokens in centralized SQL/SQLite databases.
  • Does NOT track user web browsing history, sell data to data brokers, or include advertising SDKs.
  • Does NOT use telemetry trackers or fingerprinting scripts.

4. Client-Side Data Storage & Encryption

Credentials stored on your device:

  • Web Client: Stored in your browser's private origin-bound localStorage (matrix_access_token, matrix_user_id). Other domains cannot access this sandbox.
  • Linux Desktop: Stored in ~/.config/Protutech Discord/ governed by standard Linux user permissions.
  • Windows Desktop: Stored in %APPDATA%\Protutech Discord\.

5. Your Right to Audit & Delete Data

You have absolute control over your local data. You can at any moment:

  • Use the on-page Client Storage Auditor at chat.protutech.vip/#securitySection to inspect all active keys.
  • Purge all locally cached tokens with a single click, instantly terminating the session on that device.

6. Code Transparency & Verification

We believe that privacy promises should be verifiable by code, not just legal text. The full source code of the client, server configuration, and bridge scripts is available for public audit at github.com/IAndrexI/discordapi.
