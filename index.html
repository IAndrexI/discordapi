Send any of these commands to @discordbot:chat.protutech.vip inside the client:

Command Description
login-token user <token> Authenticates using your user token. Automatically redacted.
ping Verifies connection and prints your connected username.
guilds status Lists discovered servers and their bridging states.
guilds bridge <id> --entire Bridges a specific server and all its text channels.
rejoin-space main Sends an invite to your master Space.
logout Disconnects your session and clears credentials.
